Discover Our Family Archive

Our archive is a place to preserve the photographs, stories, recordings and memories of the Blazek family — those we remember and those living today.

At its heart, this archive is a gift to the younger generations of our family, so they can know the people, places and stories that helped shape their lives. Every memory matters, from a favorite family recipe to a story that might otherwise be forgotten.

Share your holiday memories

This holiday season we’re gathering memories of Thanksgiving, Christmas and other family celebrations — recipes, photographs, traditions and stories, from years past and today. Share a favorite dish, a special gathering or a new memory.

You can attach photographs directly to your email. Original photographs are especially welcome, and you don’t need an archive account to contribute.

Share a Family Memory

Send us a story, a photograph, a correction or a memory. Include the approximate year, people and location if you know them. A little detail is a wonderful place to start.

Contributions are reviewed before being added. Please tell us how you would like to be credited and if you would prefer your contribution to remain private.
